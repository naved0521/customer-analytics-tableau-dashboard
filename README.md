# Customer Demographics & Behavior Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on conducting an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on online food delivery customer data to understand core demographics (Age, Gender, Occupation, Income) and how they correlate with customer loyalty (Repeat Orders) and satisfaction (Feedback). 

The goal of this dashboard is to provide actionable business intelligence for targeted marketing and customer retention strategies in the food delivery space.

## 💡 Key Insights & Business Recommendations

1. **Target the Student Segment:** 
   * Students represent the largest customer block (144 customers), followed by employees (94). 
   * **Recommendation:** Create budget-friendly student combo meals, "late-night study session" promotions, or partnerships with universities to boost order volume during off-peak weekdays.
   
2. **High Retention Rate:**
   * 76.14% of the customer base are repeat buyers. 
   * **Recommendation:** Implement a structured subscription model (e.g., free delivery loyalty tiers) to sustain this momentum and convert the remaining 23.86% one-time users into regular subscribers.

3. **Demographic Sweet Spot:**
   * The 21–25 age bracket represents the largest customer volume (183 customers) and shows the highest density of positive feedback. 
   * **Recommendation:** Allocate the majority of the digital advertising budget toward social platforms like Instagram and TikTok, highlighting quick delivery times and trendy local food options tailored to this age tier.
